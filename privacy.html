Support · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Vana is published by Paras Adhikary. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Data collection

Vana does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties. It includes no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated server connection.

Local preferences

Your selected companion, sound preference, lighting preference, resting state, and widget greeting state are stored on your device. The app and its widget share these settings through an iOS App Group. The habitat also uses local web storage for preferences. These settings are not sent to the developer. Device backups are controlled by your operating-system settings.

Permissions and content

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location. Models, animations, widget images, and interaction sounds are included in the app and play locally.

Support requests

If you email support, the information you choose to include is used to respond to your request. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Removing local data

You can change your saved preferences within the app. Removing Vana from your device removes its app data according to iOS settings. Restoring a device backup may restore saved preferences.

Children

Vana does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact getspaceprod@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
