Support · Privacy

A little world by your side.

Vana is an offline companion app for iPhone and iPhone Duo. A small Pokémon habitat sits beside a clock, with gentle interaction sounds and a moonlit StandBy widget.

Get help

Email getspaceprod@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Use the living habitat

Leave Vana open while docked for continuous 3D motion. Tap your companion to say hello, offer a Treat, or choose Rest. The moon button changes the habitat lighting, and the speaker button controls interaction sounds.

Add the StandBy widget

In StandBy, touch and hold a widget, authenticate if asked, open the widget picker, and choose Vana. The widget shows periodic poses; tapping the habitat opens the continuously animated app. iOS controls widget refresh timing and may require authentication for its buttons.

Sound

Sounds use your iPhone’s media volume and may play in Silent mode. Use the in-app speaker button to mute them.

Your settings

Your selected companion and preferences are saved locally. No account, subscription, or internet connection is required.
